Coordinates below are approximate normalized visual bounds from inspecting those images. They identify dominant masses/crops for asset and camera authoring; they are not a responsive HTML layout or an exact reconstructed lens. Refine the actual projected mesh corners against the original image before accepting a render.

Do not choose a familiar font/lens and make the source fit it. A primitive's front silhouette may pass a blockout while its volume/material still fails. Compare source/exported scene at equal aspect ratio in neutral clay and final lighting; inspect side views as well.

## Required typography artifact

Before binding live values, create a source-versus-candidate type sheet with all ten digits, D/H/M/S, Again, tally digits and the large title. Record actual family/license or authored atlas provenance, weight, glyph width/height, stroke contrast, terminals, tracking, baseline/angle and intended surface scale. Include the source crop beside the candidate as part of the asset gate. Named fonts in the specs are starting candidates, not acceptance.

For Gum/Fruit/Drawing, letterforms are major physical composition assets. Author an atlas or choose a licensed face with the required silhouette instead of approximating it with CSS stretching. Drawing's graphite set includes consistent pressure, construction art and all ten usable glyphs; runtime selects/reels them from real state. Static title art may be separately authored, but no atlas or scene fallback can bake a live count, deadline or preview screenshot from the generated mock.

Mount ink/type in the correct mesh UV or decal space. Printing follows drum curvature, specimen cut plane, acetate deformation or metal plate angle. Preserve the legible central region and correct contrast through the complete normal/reset reel movement. Accessible DOM values are immediate and authoritative.

## Lighting proof

Record one neutral/clay view plus the original opening final-light view, a three-quarter geometry inspection, one camera/motion frame and a close material crop. The light rig's source direction, floor/wall contact, reflective window/flags, wet/dry boundary and thickness response must agree in the actual exported scene.

Metal: diagonal room light and glints originate from the same architecture; do not paint a floor window unrelated to chrome reflections. Gum: long highlights follow connected broad folds and gathered neck. Wax: flame-driven pools/cool edges describe real volume; turn the flame light off once to expose dependence on baked scenery. Tide: foam/caustic/refraction depend on the same bed, posts and water contact. Fruit: branch/leaf/fruit/tag shadows share one sun direction. Drawing: window reflection, curl/contact and mullion falloff share one physical source.

## Phone recomposition

Source bounds above apply to the original wide opening frame only. Author portrait reading poses and actual supported arrangements; protect recognizable silhouettes and all four values rather than squeezing the entire wide picture. Desktop-versus-phone may vary crop, camera target, sequential exhibit exposure and a bounded rig pose, but cannot turn a canyon, branch or mechanism into isolated standard tiles/cards. A larger crop of scenery is preferable to smaller illegible controls.

No new geometry, glyph atlas, lighting proof or responsive scene has been produced by this document. These artifacts are explicit prerequisites of the next implementation pass.
