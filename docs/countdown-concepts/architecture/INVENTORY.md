Each show entry contains its exact version records, not a repeated generic card grid. Node IDs identify a reading surface/action; they do not prescribe uniform size, shape, support or spacing. The author uses its concept reference, rest-frame anchors and silhouette gates to model the assembly. Further versions must have visible adjoining edges/lips or named tabs and direct semantic focus; a tucked work is never permanently inaccessible.

Opening compositions preserve the original selected hierarchy. Roadworks exposes seven stations; Gum exposes four unequal hanging works; Wax/Tide/Fruit/Drawing/Metal expose the three reference arrangements. Other works continue in the same installation. All13 links exist regardless of current camera position. The full viewport is not a reason to reduce the archive.

Each of the seven additional destination links is on a named, exposed accessory surface adjacent to its show assembly. Five lead to existing archive pages with the selected theme preserved, Dexter has a film-strip/timeline equivalent, and Severance has a real outward destination.
