# Faithful archive stills

These thirteen 720×450 JPGs were captured from the actual archived sites in the local in-app browser at a 1440×900 desktop viewport on October 2, 2026. Timers use the archive's deliberately reconstructed live dates. Severance was allowed to complete its intro before capture.

They are lightweight closed-exhibit textures for the eight art themes. Opening an exhibit loads its actual page in the existing preview overlay. Royal, Control and per-show archive pages retain their existing iframe behavior. The images are not invented show art and the archived pages were not redesigned or recolored.
