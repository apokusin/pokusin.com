# Fruit replacement — working review record

First actual browser candidate: parent captured `/private/tmp/fruit-first-browser.png`.
Its source hierarchy and branch/type/paper placement were recognizable, but the
material gate failed. Both parent and an independent Metal/Roadworks agent found
hollow pendant/mirror readings, thin peel/pith, uniformly pale flesh, hose-like
bark with pale dots, pristine purple eggs, overly stiff/dark leaves and heavy
destination tags. No acceptance was granted.

The following pass replaces flat pulp protection with four unequal longitudinal
lobes and binds the true reel ink to the same curved reading skin. It broadens
the individual cut lemon silhouettes and uneven peel/pith; separates moist pulp
from dry pore-covered rind; adds real local pulp AO baked independently of direct
light and albedo. Wood now has modeled knots, torn cambium plates and correlated
moss/brown/scar/fissure maps. Figs have irregular outline and striped cracked
skin. Leaves have nicked/dusty edges, tip twist and lighter veining. The real press
tally hangs visibly beside the reset with an actual cotton backing. Destination
marks use small arrow folds with generous non-shadowing native-link proxies.

The clock uses the requested heavier condensed numeral silhouette. All13
individual works have a scroll stop, physical approach and exact semantic twin;
all7 extra destinations preserve genuine default browser link behavior.

Source-ray clearance now passes81 samples for all4 actual curved clocks,13 real
archives and7 extra links at the two tested aspect ratios. This is geometry
evidence, not actual browser/input/performance/art acceptance. Parent must
compare the updated full browser frame and390/320 reading views before granting
the fidelity gate. Microscopic detail remains a surface approximation; no
volumetric botany, cloth simulation or photographed scene is being claimed.
