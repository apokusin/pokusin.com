# Held in Suspense

An authored mechanical installation with closed beveled folded arms, true
articulated joints, braided load/suspension cables, a fractured slate anchor,
tall unequal clock cassettes and an oblique polished cylinder. Architecture in
reflection_room supplies real light/dark shapes for chrome. Printed faces are

## Reproduce

Use Blender **4.5.14 LTS**, build 62c1db4208e8, from the repository root:

```sh
Blender --background --python countdowns/assets/concepts/held-in-suspense/scene/authoring/create_scene.py
Blender --background --python countdowns/assets/concepts/held-in-suspense/scene/authoring/create_scene.py -- --phone
python3 docs/countdown-concepts/architecture/measure_assets.py
```

The two runs save held-in-suspense.blend and held-in-suspense-mobile.blend,
their separate GLBs/manifests and real lit/clay inspections. The phone rig keeps
actual cassette proportions, places the weight beside/below the readable row,
and routes its load behind the photographic apertures through real rear shafts.
It is not a crop of the desktop camera. Desktop inspection/optional fallback
conversion is also available in authoring/inspect_export.py; the shared budget
helper measures both exports without changing their model or manifests.

## Coordinates, surfaces and mechanics

Blender Z-up maps to glTF (x,z,-y), with Y vertical and +Z forward. Each manifest
records the exact exported camera FOV, target, reading-face sizes and cap basis.
clock_d/h/m/s have genuine roughly 1:2.19 faces and project taller to the right.
reset_ink is a true circular endcap skin perpendicular to the cylinder axis;
reset_tally uses the same upright tangent basis. The cap’s metal volume, static
word/people mark and live slot tally share one real reset action.

Three unequal opening mounts are authored as archive_dexter_mount,
archive_severance_mount and archive_got_mount, each with its own named screen.
Runtime extends the same beveled mount geometry into all thirteen real works;
version cassettes fan on connected supports. The native route includes each
record and seven real accessory destinations. Additional mounts are runtime
geometry, not thirteen distinct meshes claimed to be inside the base GLB.

The browser derives clear reading positions from actual solid intersections.
inspection/geometry-clearance.json records 81 aperture rays per work, desktop,
390/320 composition measurements, exact frozen camera/fan/filings/mechanical
restore and immediate reduced-motion pressure/flange checks. These are geometric
and behavioral evidence; they do not certify browser light, readability or art.

Colour PNGs are sRGB; normal/roughness maps are linear data. Small directional
brushing is microdetail. Slate clefts, lifted flakes, joints, openings and body
curvature are modeled relief. Narrow satin finishing bands surround the actual
polished body; reflection gradients are supplied by room geometry. Window panes
do not cast shadows, while physical mullions do. Geometry/maps are authored in
the project, with no new license declaration here. Shared font/runtime notices
and exact measured bundle hashes are indexed in ../../README.md.

The browser reflection rig includes the authored floor/back wall and completes
the plaster side returns/ceiling. The general room is captured at the sculpture
center (256px cube faces), with a separate local capture at the inclined weight
(512px cube faces) so the endcap reflects the floor and the body crosses the
actual window/mullion bays. Both captures are static; the convolution work
buffers are released after baking. No reference raster is used as an environment.
