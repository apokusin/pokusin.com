# After the Flame

An editable poured wax landscape with connected banks, rooted folds, real
Boolean reading recesses, candle/wick, shallow pressure bowl and bounded molten
films. The desktop/phone GLBs contain actual sculpted volume and separate blank
live-state/photo surfaces. Runtime owns daylight, flame response, ink, faithful
archive stills and the real shared reset; the asset does not calculate a timer.

Detailed reproduction and clearance evidence are in
[authoring/README.md](authoring/README.md). Use Blender 4.5.14 LTS; retain the
separate scene.blend and scene-mobile.blend. The portrait rig includes an
independently authored clock bank and grounded pressure support. Do not decimate
the cavity-bearing bank blindly: the rejected earlier LOD closed real apertures.

Colour, pore normal, roughness, thickness approximation and independently baked
contact AO have separate jobs. Reading beds remain inside the actual recesses.
Closed-mass/topology and 81-ray/0.16m swept navigation checks prove geometric
clearance; they do not establish browser material fidelity or visual acceptance.
The current exact export sizes/hashes are in the shared asset-budgets.json report;
its embedded-image count excludes the separately loaded 1024px crevice AO.

Geometry and procedural maps are project-authored; this README introduces no
new license declaration. The earlier canyon.webp is retained reference artwork,
not the source of this model or a full photo backdrop. ../provenance.json keeps
its exact ImageGen prompt, original output identifier and converted WebP hash.
Blank-face authoring proofs remain distinct from real browser previews.
