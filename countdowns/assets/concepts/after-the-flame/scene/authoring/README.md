# Wax landscape authoring

This asset is an editable, project-authored wax landscape. It uses no scenery photograph, traced mockup, baked countdown value or altered archive screenshot.

Run Blender **4.5.14 LTS** with:

```
Blender --background --python build_scene.py -- --proofs
```

Then run `check_navigation.py`, `export_mobile.py`, `inspect_export.py`,
`check_navigation.py -- --mobile-verify`, and `check_geometry.py` with the
same Blender executable (the export inspector alone uses normal Python).
`render_current.py` refreshes inspection renders after a geometry correction.
No website build or additional runtime dependency is introduced.

The script creates a poured channel with deliberately authored height fields; tapered hollow candle walls; connected sculpted banks; genuine Boolean reading cavities; unequal rooted melt folds; thin molten films; a shallow pressure pool; and one brass crown. The joined voxel sculpt provides continuous intersections. Its fine folds are real geometry; pore normal, cooled/wet roughness and independently baked crevice occlusion are separate channels.

Blank UV reading meshes receive the authoritative shared timer, genuine archive screenshots and sparse labels at runtime. `Return_uphill`, `Pressure` and `Restored_tip` morphs are bounded material responses. The runtime does not simulate fluids or calculate another deadline.

`scene.blend` preserves the authored geometry, material graph, lights and artist camera. `scene.glb` is served directly, without a site build. The inspection renders are asset proofs, **not browser acceptance**. Browser light/material fidelity and phone compositions must be checked separately against the original concept.

Current limits: surface transmission approximates thin wax, without volumetric scattering; the candidate uses an authored voxel/analytic sculpt, rather than a scanned candle. Generated reference screenshots are replaced with faithful real archive stills. No final visual acceptance is implied by export success.

The opening plus all 13 real records are separate native scroll stops. Each
record has desktop, 390px and 320px measured camera poses in `manifest.json`.
`navigation-clearance.json` checks 81 rays reaching to 1.5% from each image
edge, clear access from the channel, and a 0.16m swept camera path between
every stop. The phone report loads the saved `scene-mobile.blend` itself and repeats the checks against its actual bank, rooted pressure bowl and LOD geometry.
Later image beds remain shallow inside the genuine sculpted aperture mouth;
the first three retain the deeper original arrangement. One later GoT terrace
is farther right to avoid burying it in the original rising backwall.

Plain decimation of the cavity-bearing terrain closed photo apertures and
was rejected. The phone bundle preserves that already-budgeted sculpt and
reduces only the closed candle skin. Desktop is 11,004,008 bytes / 188,898
triangles; phone is 10,856,748 bytes / 180,458 triangles, including its own poured clock bank and closed pressure platform. Both retain UVs,
normals, tangents and eight bounded morph meshes. Embedded maps decode to
4,194,304 RGBA bytes; the separately loaded 1024px crevice AO adds 4,194,304.

The exact exported camera FOV is 42.421543 degrees. This matches the saved
render lens/aspect; Blender's generic sensor-height angle was narrower and
does not describe the wide authored frame. Dynamic clock/tally ink remains
independent. The tally is a transparent slot-reel skin on the curved pressure
pool, carrying its own pressure morph without a separate floating label.

The portrait artist source is `scene-mobile.blend`. Its camera is centered at
glTF `[-3.25,10,27]` toward `[-3.25,3.8,5.2]`, with a 42° vertical lens. The
pressure pool's complete Basis and Pressure coordinates are translated to
Blender `[-3.25,-8.7,0.6]`; its closed unequal poured support intersects the
existing floor roots. A continuous local floor deformation makes the actual
shallow concavity while preserving the closed original archive topology.
Phone Again ink is larger and the real tally reels use a compact, adaptive UV
window on the same pressure surface. Desktop files and behavior are retained.

Current offline results: all 13 photo sightlines and every 0.16m swept rail
are clear at desktop, 390px and 320px. The actual portrait source also has
zero blocked samples across each clock's glyph/unit region and across Again
and tally at rest and during pending pressure. The top border row of each
lower reading cavity has nine blocked samples outside the ink region; its
physical upper lip remains. Mass, portrait bank and pressure support each
have zero boundary/non-manifold edges. These reports prove geometric
clearance, not lighting fidelity, motion feel or browser polish.
